# Phase 5 — Reliability, Observability & History

**Status:** Planned
**Owner:** Yomu app
**Milestone:** #15

## Goal
Make repeated translation sessions diagnosable, recoverable, and useful over time.

## Scope
Structured diagnostics, privacy-safe logs, cancellation/timeouts, persistence and history quality, context safety, and recovery from interrupted or partial work.

## Exit criteria
Common failures leave actionable evidence, sessions do not silently lose results, and history accurately represents completed and failed work.

## Dependencies and issue ownership
Builds on the Phase 1 pipeline and Phase 3 model lifecycle. Reliability, observability, and history issues belong to Yomu.
